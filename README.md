# TryHackMe Walkthroughs

Welcome to my walkthroughs for [TryHackMe](https://tryhackme.com/) rooms, challenges and learning paths. You can find my profile [here](https://tryhackme.com/p/Ungarssympa).

I'm Clément, based in France, holding the eJPT and eCPPT certifications. My long-term goal is offensive security (pentest / red team), with a first step as a SOC analyst. These walkthroughs are mainly documentation for myself, a way to retain what I learn, but I hope they will help others too.

## Languages

My walkthroughs are written in **English and French**. French is my native language, so it is where I write most naturally, and I provide English versions to reach a wider audience. Each walkthrough indicates which languages are available (🇬🇧 / 🇫🇷). Some exist in only one language for now, and I add the missing translations over time.

## Walkthrough format

Each walkthrough follows the same structure:

1. **Overview**: what the room is about, difficulty, available languages
2. **Reconnaissance**: scanning and enumeration
3. **Exploitation**: how I got the foothold
4. **Post-exploitation**: privilege escalation and further steps
5. **Lessons learned**: what I take away from the room

## When to use

These walkthroughs are meant for two situations.

**Scenario #1: you are stuck.**
You have already tried several approaches on your own and don't know how to continue. Don't open a walkthrough too early though: struggling is part of learning, and developing a "try harder" mindset is worth it.

**Scenario #2: you already solved the room.**
You want to compare your method with mine, discover other tools, or find a smarter way to get the same result.

## Ethics

Everything documented here was done inside TryHackMe's legal lab environment. Never use these techniques on systems you do not own or do not have explicit written permission to test.

## Language disclaimer

Even though French is my native language, I do my best in English too, but you may find a few spelling or grammar mistakes in the English versions. Feel free to open an issue or a pull request if you spot one.
